# Surface foundation stiffness sensitivity

A unit solid bar is supported on a consistent surface foundation and loaded on its far face. The normal foundation stiffness is differentiated directly and is also identified jointly with Young's modulus from two static observations.

The independent reference is the Winkler equilibrium u(0)=p/k and u(L)=p/k+pL/E. Thus the support derivative is -p/k² and the two observations identify E and k without a fitted numerical reference. This is a small command-level check of the face foundation route.
