# Open contact perturbation

Two unit-area elastic layers are separated by a 0.02 m gap and carry no preload. The `open` perturbation policy removes contact stiffness. The independent reduced matrices give three shear eigenvalues as the positive roots of `((z−15016)z + 27168000)z − 108000000 = 0` and one axial eigenvalue `z = 3000`; each frequency is `sqrt(z)/(2π)`. The polynomial comes from the explicitly stated layer mass and shear matrices, so no solver result supplies the reference.
