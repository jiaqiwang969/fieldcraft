# Damped single-degree-of-freedom free decay

The oscillator has m=1, k=4 and Rayleigh mass damping c=0.4. It starts at u=1 and v=-0.2, so the exact response is a cosine with exponentially decaying peaks. The time step is one thousandth of the damped period. The measured ratio of two successive positive peaks is exp(delta), the exponential form of the logarithmic decrement.
