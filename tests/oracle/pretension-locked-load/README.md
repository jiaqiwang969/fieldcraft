# Locked pretension with an external centre load

Two one-metre, unit-area axial segments have `E1 = 1000 Pa` and `E2 = 2000 Pa`, fixed at their outer ends. A 10 N pretension is applied at the internal cut, then its adjustment is locked. A further 3 N force is applied at the common centre plane. The preload opening is `q = 10(1/1000 + 1/2000) = 0.015 m`.

After locking, let the lower and upper centre displacements be `y` and `z`, with `y − z = q`; the outer-end reactions give `σ1 − σ2 = 3 Pa`, while the locked compatibility gives `(σ1 − 10)/1000 + (σ2 − 10)/2000 = 0`. Solving gives `σ1 = 11 Pa` and `σ2 = 8 Pa`; these are the independent segment stresses.
